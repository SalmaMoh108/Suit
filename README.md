# Suit
**Everyone lies on their resume. Your job is to catch them.**


**Play on Itch:** [Suit](https://jesterstudio.itch.io/suit)

A small project developed for a university presentation.

## Visuals

![SUIT Cover Image](pic.png)

## Core Mechanics
* **Tactical Interrogation Loop:** Review candidate profiles and select strategic dialogue options to pressure applicants, separating industry experts from complete frauds.
* **Directional Swiping Mechanics:** Keep the hiring pipeline moving with rapid-fire decisions. Click the check on the candidate card to Hire, or the cross to Fire.
* **Terminal UI Aesthetic:** A sleek, corporate database interface featuring dynamic typewriter text effects, smooth alpha-fade screen transitions, and satisfying physical stamp feedback.
* **Executive Grading System:** Performance is meticulously tracked and evaluated at the end of the shift, awarding a final grade (A-F) based on the overall accuracy of the player's hiring decisions.
